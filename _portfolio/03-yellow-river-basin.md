As a research assistant at Northwest Normal University from March to June 2024, I worked in a four-person team to construct ecological and economic indicators for the Yellow River Basin. We used InVEST to estimate ecosystem service value and applied coupling coordination and raster-level spatial autocorrelation analyses to examine the spatial relationships among the indicators.

I also contributed to an ArcGIS Engine platform that visualized the resulting regional patterns.
