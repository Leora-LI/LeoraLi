As a research assistant in the [3MSI Lab](https://www.3msi.net/) at The Hong Kong Polytechnic University, I work with [Prof. Tiangang Yin](https://www.polyu.edu.hk/lsgs/people/academic-staff/prof-tiangang-yin/) on reconstructing forest structure from airborne LiDAR. I integrated NASA G-LiHT point clouds, terrain data, and FoScenes plant area density products across eight forest plots at the Smithsonian Environmental Research Center. I used canopy height, return intensity, point density, and plant area density to model how the woody fraction varies with height.

I developed a method that allocates predicted woody area across canopy layers and voxels while preserving each tree's predicted total. On held-out trees, the best allocation performed better than direct voxel-level random forest predictions when evaluated against a reference derived from leaf-off LiDAR.
