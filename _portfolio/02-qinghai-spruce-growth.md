For my 2025 BSc thesis at Northwest Normal University, I combined Qinghai spruce tree-ring width chronologies from 1988 to 2018 with GIMMS3g and MODIS Normalized Difference Vegetation Index (NDVI) and climate records. I processed satellite data in R and Google Earth Engine and used Pearson and Spearman correlations to examine seasonal associations at 2,600 m and 2,800 m elevation.

The analysis found stronger associations between radial growth and early-season precipitation and later-season temperature. Associations between radial growth and satellite NDVI were weak at both elevations.
