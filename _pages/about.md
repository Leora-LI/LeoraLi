I am an MSc student in Geomatics at The Hong Kong Polytechnic University and a research assistant in the [3MSI Lab](https://www.3msi.net/). I work with [Prof. Tiangang Yin](https://www.polyu.edu.hk/lsgs/people/academic-staff/prof-tiangang-yin/) on estimating the three-dimensional distribution of woody area in forest canopies using airborne LiDAR collected during the leaf-on season.

I earned my BSc in Geographic Information Science from Northwest Normal University. For my undergraduate thesis, I combined tree-ring records, satellite observations, and climate data to study what drives Qinghai spruce growth in the Helan Mountains.

Research Interests
======

- GeoAI and machine learning for environmental monitoring
- Airborne LiDAR and 3D reconstruction of forest structure
- Spatiotemporal prediction and integration of satellite and ecological observations
